# Licensing Rules

Before redistribution, document and verify:

- commercial-use rights
- redistribution rights
- modification or quantization rights
- attribution requirements
- notice requirements
- an archived copy of the source license
- the exact archived source revision

Commercial-use permission does not automatically include redistribution
permission. Likewise, access to public model weights does not establish the
right to republish them.

Review the terms that apply to the exact revision and every bundled dependency.
Record limitations as well as permissions. If modification is prohibited, do
not convert or quantize the model. If any required right is unclear, keep the
entry `planned` or `evaluating`; it must not become `ready`.

Each ready pack must point to its archived license and attribution files. Keep
required notices with the package and in the repository's licensing record.
Legal metadata must be based on source evidence and must never be guessed.
